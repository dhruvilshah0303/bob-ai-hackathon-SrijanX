FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How does AURA calculate total time-to-treatment?
AURA combines real-time traffic GPS drive times with receiving hospital ER bay occupancy, ICU bed availability, and specialist shift duty to estimate true time until a patient sees a doctor.
Can paramedics override the AI recommendation?
Yes. Paramedics and dispatchers always maintain final authority. Overrides are logged with a brief rationale for compliance and clinical audit.
Is AURA HIPAA compliant?
Absolustely. AURA encrypts all telemetry in transit and at rest, operating under strict HIPAA compliance standards and ISO-27001 data security guidelines.
